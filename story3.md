# The Wizard of Normal Mode

In the kingdom of Bufferheim, where parchment scrolled endlessly and cursors blinked like fireflies, there lived a wizard named Vimothy the Magnificent. He wore a robe embroidered with `:wq` on the back, and his pointed hat bore the sigil of a blinking block cursor.

Unlike other wizards who waved wands and shouted incantations, Vimothy cast his spells with *keystrokes*.

One stormy evening, a dragon descended upon the village of Insertshire. The townsfolk screamed and fled. Vimothy strode forward, cracked his knuckles, and muttered, **"Normal mode... engaged."**

He pressed `gg` and teleported instantly to the front of the battlefield. The dragon roared. Vimothy replied by smashing `daw` — *delete a word* — and the beast's terrifying war cry vanished from the air, leaving an awkward silence. The dragon blinked, confused.

"But I'm not done," Vimothy whispered with a grin.

He typed `ciw` — *change inner word* — and replaced the dragon's fiery breath with a gentle spring breeze. Flowers bloomed at its nostrils. The dragon sneezed a bouquet of daisies.

The crowd gasped. A child in the back shouted, **"How?!"**

"Muscle memory," Vimothy called back without turning around.

The dragon, now humiliated and slightly hay-fevered, attempted a counter-attack. It lunged. Vimothy calmly pressed `u` — *undo* — and the dragon lurched backwards in time, sitting back down on its haunches as if the lunge had never happened.

"You can't undo *me!*" the dragon bellowed.

"Ctrl-R," said Vimothy, and *redid* an elaborate victory dance he hadn't technically done yet. It was confusing for everyone, but it looked incredible.

For the finishing move, Vimothy raised both hands dramatically, typed `:%s/dragon/friendly lizard/g`, and pressed Enter. A shimmer rippled across the beast. Where the fearsome dragon once stood, there was now a small, smiling lizard wearing a tiny scarf.

The village erupted in cheers.

As Vimothy walked back toward his tower, an apprentice ran up beside him. "Master! What is the most powerful spell you know?"

Vimothy paused. He looked off into the distance, the wind catching his `:wq` robe.

"Exiting without saving," he said quietly, "when someone else's unsaved changes are in the buffer."

The apprentice shuddered.

"But," Vimothy added, eyes twinkling, "a wise wizard always types `:wqa` and sleeps peacefully."

He disappeared into his tower, and from the window above, a single light blinked — once, twice — like a cursor waiting patiently for the next command.

*And in Bufferheim, all was well. Until someone accidentally pressed `ZZ` in the wrong buffer.*
